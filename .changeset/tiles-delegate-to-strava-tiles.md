Delegate the tiles to `strava-tiles`, which works without Gatsby too. The options, the `tiles` fields, the `gatsby-strava-tiles/card` entry and the `gatsby-strava-tiles/utils/map` and `gatsby-strava-tiles/utils/tiles` entries stay the same. The main entry also exports `computeTiles`, `getAthleteTiles`, `getRouteTiles` and `readRoutes`.
